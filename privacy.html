Privacy Policy for Revasri Thermal Vision

Effective Date: September 30, 2026
Developer: Adam Outler (adamoutler@gmail.com)

Revasri Thermal Vision — Paid / Pro Edition (com.adamoutler.thermal_camera)

The Paid Edition is completely air-gapped. It does not request INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, or AD_ID permissions, contains zero analytics or advertising SDKs, and processes all thermal imagery strictly on-device.

Revasri Thermal Vision — Free Edition (com.adamoutler.thermal_camera.free)

The Free Edition is supported by Google AdMob. It requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions strictly to request and display advertisements. Google AdMob may process pseudonymous identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID, device metadata, coarse IP-derived location, and ad interaction metrics. Thermal camera streams, radiometry data, and captured snapshots are never shared with advertising SDKs.

USB Device Access

Both editions use Android USB Host permissions solely to communicate with compatible microbolometer hardware (0x04b4:0x000a).
